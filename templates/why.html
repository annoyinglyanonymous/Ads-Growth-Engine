A CPA change is almost never one thing: it splits into a RATE effect (an ad got dearer) and a MIX effect (budget moved toward an ad that already was), which are different problems with different fixes. WHAT CHANGED, AND WHY The account edits used to sit at the bottom of this page. They are now first, because a budget, status or creative edit is a CAUSE and the rate/mix split is arithmetic -- read in the other order, every edit gets re-explained as creative decay. CLAUDE.md's rule about reading structure changes before blaming creative is this ordering. The split is drawn with a third bar, the ads that converted in only one of the two windows, so the bars add up to the net. Without it a reader sums rate and mix, gets a different number, and trusts neither. One 40-row table became two short ones -- what pushed CPA up, and what pulled it down -- because those are the two questions somebody arrives with, and a single table sorted by size of effect mixes them. ===================================================================== -#} {% block toolbar %}
