Every other page answers one question; this one is the whole account, read in the order a person has to read it. WHAT THIS PAGE LEARNED FROM BEING READ It used to render nine numbered sections at full fidelity whatever they contained. Against this account that meant roughly three thousand of its five and a half thousand pixels were empty boxes -- the angle bank is inert, nothing is tagged, no experiment has been filed -- and the first row naming an ad somebody could act on today sat about nineteen hundred pixels down. Meanwhile the six sentences the rules had already written were scattered one per section, in six places, where nobody read them in order. So: FINDINGS FIRST, EVIDENCE UNDER THEM, AND A SECTION EARNS ITS HEIGHT. * The readings are promoted into one ranked band at the top, grouped by the section they belong to, each with a miniature carrying the shape of the claim and a link to the table that proves it. * A section with no rows collapses to one line that says WHY it is empty. "It loaded and the answer is none" and "it could not be read" are different claims, and an empty box says neither. Opening the line still gives the whole section. * A long table shows five rows and then says what is in the tail, so the reader knows the shape of it without scrolling it. Nothing was deleted. Every section is still here and every number still comes out of the same function. THE ONE RULE THIS TEMPLATE EXISTS TO ENFORCE A fact and a reading are never allowed to look alike. CLAUDE.md puts it plainly -- "the numbers a function returns are facts, your reading of them is an opinion" -- and a page that renders an opinion inside a table cell has quietly promoted it to a measurement. So: facts live in .kpi tiles, .stat tiles, tables and charts, and a table names the SQL that produced it beside its heading. readings live in the findings band, labelled as readings of the figures below and never inside a table cell. proposals get a third label -- "Proposal -- not filed" -- and the command that would file them, so the reader can see for themselves that nothing has been filed. They are told apart by PLACE and SHAPE, not by colour. Exactly one finding row is ever tinted, and only when it is about the data rather than about the advertising: colour on the rest would make an opinion read as an alarm, and the difference between the two is the entire point. WHAT IS NOT PRINTED HERE, AND WHERE IT WENT Rule names, /facts pointers and provisional flags are not on the page. They are still on every reading, readings.py still refuses to let a rule state a figure it cannot cite, and briefs/-.json still holds the whole chain. The audit trail is for the person CHECKING the brief, not the person reading it, and printing both to one page made the second job harder. ===================================================================== -#} {#- THE CHIPS SAY WHICH DOCUMENT THIS IS.
