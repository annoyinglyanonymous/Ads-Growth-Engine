The previous palette was blue-only, and its rule -- severity by intensity, direction by arrow -- existed because one hue cannot say "good". Agency Heights ships success and error families, so they are used. The arrows STAY: colour is the second channel, never the only one, or the 8% of men with a red-green deficiency read a rising CPA as a win. And one rule that outranks both palettes, in `.delta` below: in advertising, up is not good. Spend up is neither. CPA up is bad. Conversions up is good.
