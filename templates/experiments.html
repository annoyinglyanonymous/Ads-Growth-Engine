WHAT CHANGED, AND WHY Five tiles say the state of the whole pipeline at once: filed, running, waiting on a conclusion, ideas, and findings that are not a test at all. The ideas were seven tall cards, each with its own before/after table, copy and brief. They are now one row each -- the idea, where it came from, what moved as small chips, and whether it can be filed -- and every row opens to the full card, so nothing a writer needed is gone. The ordering rule is kept: what has been decided outranks what has been suggested. When the log has experiments it comes first; when it is empty, as it is today, it folds into one line at the bottom rather than opening the page with an empty table. ONE FLOW WITH /suggestions (2026-10-02). The report recommends a test and names its tag; the person launches the new ad with utm_content=; the outcomes ledger sees the launch and scripts/suggest.py files the experiment as a proposal, pre-registered from the plan published before the launch. A proposal whose launch was seen shows the date and the exact statement that records started_on. That statement is here, in the template, and nowhere in intel/: it is run by the person who launched the ad, against the database, and nothing on this site runs it.
